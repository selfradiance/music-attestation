A public catalog of Zion Skank recordings that have been cryptographically sealed. Browse the music first; open any track to inspect its proof.

What the seal means

A seal signs the exact bytes of an audio file. If a copy produces the same SHA-256 hash and its signature verifies with the public key, it matches the sealed recording. This does not independently establish authorship, copyright ownership, audio quality, or a trusted creation time.
